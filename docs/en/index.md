## What sound and lighting engineering means

At its core, sound and lighting engineering covers the equipment and techniques used in stage and related arts for **creating, storing, transmitting and modifying sound**, and for controlling the **brightness, colour and form of light** — in other words, using science and technique to control sound and light. Only when it follows the situation, mood and emotion of the performance does it come alive and count as design or art. In short, it is a work of art built on technology.

Whatever the scale of an event, success depends on participation, organisation and a sensible division of work. Sound and lighting mistakes are obvious during a show and can easily spoil it, so besides understanding the content of the performance you need to know the ever-changing equipment and combine what you learn with your own sense of aesthetics.

Another key purpose is to **imitate nature**, especially its sound and light. You cannot produce a night street scene on an open-air stage in daylight, but you can easily control it inside a theatre. By controlling sound and light, the audience's emotions can be brought in step with the performance.

## Equipment and what it does

| Category | Equipment |
| --- | --- |
| Audio (create, transmit, store, modify sound) | Mixer, echo unit, equalizer, power amplifier, pre-amplifier, speaker, microphone |
| Lighting (brightness, colour, presentation) | Spotlights (PAR64, PC500, LEKO, Fresnel), floodlights (cyc lights, colour lights), special-effect units (strobe, mirror ball, bubble machine, fog machine) |

The more equipment you know and understand, the more it helps your later work. Watch and listen to stage shows; observing builds creativity and sparks ideas. Taking part and doing things hands-on is indispensable.
